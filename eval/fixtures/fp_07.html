New Quantum Processor Achieves 99.9% Fidelity

Published: 2026-09-24 14:30:15 UTC
Article ID: 9872346102

In a major breakthrough, researchers have successfully demonstrated a 256-qubit processor that maintains quantum coherence for over 12000 milliseconds.

This development beats the previous record set by device SN-4815162342 which could only manage 4500 milliseconds. The new architecture uses a novel error-correction grid, patent pending under US-1122334455.

The total investment for the project was $1,500,000.00, funded by the Global Science Foundation (grant number GSF-2026-8877665544).
